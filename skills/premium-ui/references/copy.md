# Words as design: headlines and page copy

On top-tier sites the words are part of the composition: short, concrete,
set big. Weak copy ("Geleceği birlikte kuralım", "Dijital dönüşümün
öncüsü") makes a strong layout look like a template. `craft-baseline.md`
§4 has the microcopy rules; this file is for headlines and scene text.

## 1. Find the words in the product

Before writing, collect 10–15 phrases the product and its users already
use: README, UI labels, config names, support texts, the founder's own
description, competitor gaps. Headlines come from these, not from a
slogan generator.

## 2. Headline patterns that work

| Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- |
| The result, with the real number | "Kargo kodu 15 dakikada müşterinde." |
| The visitor's question, answered | "Siparişin nerede? Artık sen de biliyorsun." |
| Before → after | "Üç panel yerine tek liste." |
| The object of the product | "Her paket, bir satır." |
| A plain claim + one twist word | "Stok eşit. **Her** pazaryerinde." |

Rules:

- 2–7 words for display headlines, one idea, no comma chains.
- One emphasis device per headline (the direction's `--em-*`), on the
  word that carries the meaning, not on a filler word.
- Turkish word order is a tool: the important word goes last for
  emphasis ("Gecikmeyi ilk sen gör.").
- Verbs over nouns ("bağla", "gör", "öde"), concrete nouns over abstract
  ones ("paket", "masa", "fatura" over "çözüm", "deneyim", "değer").
- Numbers only from the project (config, DB, user).

## 3. Banned (unless the product literally is it)

"geleceği", "dijital dönüşüm", "yenilikçi çözümler", "tek platformda her
şey", "sınırları zorla", "fark yarat", "bir adım önde", "kusursuz deneyim",
"güçlü", "akıllı", "yeni nesil", "inovatif", "seamless", "next-level",
"unlock", "elevate", emoji in headlines, three adjectives in a row.

## 4. The scene text stack

Each scene has at most: eyebrow (2–3 words, often a number: "02 — Konum"),
headline, one sentence of body (≤ 25 words), one action. More text goes to
a detail page or an expandable list, not the scene.

## 5. Check

Read the page's headlines alone, top to bottom, as a list. They should
tell the product's story without the rest of the page, and none of them
could sit on a competitor's site unchanged (the swap test).
